How we build

Five clear steps from plot to possession

  1. 1

    Site visit

    We inspect the plot, soil and access, and listen to what you need.

  2. 2

    Design & estimate

    Plans, elevations and an itemised cost estimate you can review line by line.

  3. 3

    Approvals

    We prepare drawings and support you through the local plan sanction.

  4. 4

    Construction

    Stage-wise execution with quality checks and regular progress updates.

  5. 5

    Handover

    Final walkthrough, snag fixing and handover of keys and documents.
